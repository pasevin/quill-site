From your Mac to your reMarkable,
before you've looked up.

A native macOS menu-bar app that turns markdown into a native reMarkable notebook — with articles, PDFs and EPUBs alongside. Built locally, sent directly.

In development — public release soon
Features

Everything reaches the tablet

One tool for every way an idea arrives — a note you're writing, an article you're reading, a paper you've downloaded.

✍️

Markdown → native notebook

Real typed text with headings, bullets and prose — not a screenshot. Edit and reorganize it on the tablet like anything else you've written.

🔗

URL → article

Paste a link; Quill extracts the article and delivers it as a clean, readable notebook. Table paginated so it stays legible at tablet width.

📄

PDF & EPUB, raw

Documents upload as themselves — no conversion, no reflow, no fidelity loss. They open in the tablet viewer and on my.remarkable.com.

♻️

Update in place

Re-push a note and the existing document updates — no duplicate pile-up. Deletes work the same clean way.

📂

Into the right folder

Choose the destination folder per push from a live folder tree, or let the watched inbox folder file things for you.

⌥⌘R

One keystroke away

A global hotkey pushes whatever's on your clipboard. A Finder Quick Action and a global hotkey push selected text from any app.

Privacy

Your words stay yours

Quill was designed from the start so that nothing about your content passes through anyone else's servers.

◆

Local by construction. Documents are prepared and uploaded from your Mac, straight to your own reMarkable account. There is no Quill server in the path.

◆

No account with us. Quill pairs with your reMarkable device once, using reMarkable's own pairing flow. We never see your credentials.

◆

Works on free accounts. The cloud sync protocol is used directly — no Connect subscription required for any feature.

◆

Open foundation. Built on the open-source reMarkable sync protocol work of the community.
