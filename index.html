The Problem

Getting work onto a reMarkable shouldn't mean freezing it into PDFs.

The reMarkable is the best distraction-free reading and thinking surface ever made — until you try to send a Markdown spec, a long web essay, or a revised draft from your Mac.

Without Quill
  • ×Frozen output. Print-to-PDF locks your Markdown into static pages — you can't edit text or reflow paragraphs on the tablet.
  • ×Duplicate clutter. Pushing an updated draft creates a second copy alongside the old one every single time.
  • ×Broken tables & articles. Wide Markdown tables clip off the margin; web printouts drag navbars and ads onto e-ink.
  • ×Connect-gated deletes. Most CLI tools rely on legacy endpoints that return 401 on free accounts.
With Quill
  • ✓Native editable notebooks. Markdown compiles directly to reMarkable v6 typed text — headings, lists, and prose stay editable on device.
  • ✓Updates in place. Re-pushing a note replaces the previous version cleanly via sync-v3 soft-delete. One note, always current.
  • ✓Readable tables & articles. Tables auto-paginate into crisp raster chunks; bare URLs extract clean article text before upload.
  • ✓Free-account native. Push, raw PDF/EPUB send, and cloud delete all run over sync v3 — no Connect tier needed.

    Architecture & Privacy

    Direct from your Mac to your tablet. Nobody in the middle.

    Every document conversion, table rasterization, and sync-v3 upload happens locally on your Mac. There is no Quill backend, no tracking, and no account to create.

    Zero server footprint

    Your notes, articles, and PDFs never touch an intermediary server. Everything is packaged on your Mac and sent straight to your own reMarkable cloud.

    Official pairing flow

    Quill pairs once using reMarkable's standard 8-character device code. Credentials stay local on your machine.

    Open protocol foundation

    Built on the community's open-source Rust sync-v3 tooling, with upstreamed patches for free-account deletes, raw uploads, and current cPages metadata.

    Known platform limits

    What the reMarkable v6 format and free-tier API do not support today — stated upfront so there are no surprises.

    Tables are rasterized reMarkable v6 typed text has no native table primitive. Quill renders tables cleanly as paginated PNGs (5 rows per chunk) so they stay sharp and readable.
    Folder creation on free plans Creating new folders remotely still requires Connect; create folders on your tablet and Quill routes pushes into them automatically.
    Raw PDF/EPUB updates Update-in-place applies to Markdown pushes. Raw PDF and EPUB sends always create a new document so existing annotations are never overwritten.
    Typed-text styling v6 typed text supports headings, bullets, and prose in Latin script; inline bold/italic is not yet exposed in the native typed-text layer.
